Who Owns Sears Now, and How Many Sears Stores Are Left?

By Dana Reyes · Updated November 26, 2026 · First published September 9, 2026 · 8 min read

Sears is owned by Transform Holdco LLC (Transformco), the private company Eddie Lampert's ESL Investments formed in February 2019 to buy the chain out of bankruptcy. As of October 2026, five full-line Sears stores were left in the United States, in Miami, Orlando, Concord, Braintree and El Paso, down from 547 in February 2018.

How many Sears stores are left in 2026?

As of November 2026, five full-line Sears department stores are left in the United States: the same five USA Today counted in November 2025, listed again by the retail site SprawlTag on October 26, 2026. They sit in four states, and two of them are in Florida.

Each is the last of its kind somewhere: the last Sears in California, the last in New England, the last in Texas. Two have already given up floor space, with Primark taking part of the Braintree store in 2017 and part of the El Paso store in 2025, according to SprawlTag.

Transformco, the private owner, publishes no store directory and gives no notice before a closing, so every count here comes from reporters counting doors. The oldest of the five is also the one with a demolition plan already drawn up.

Where is the last Sears, and how fast did the rest go?

The longest survivor is the freestanding store at 3655 Coral Way in Miami, opened in 1954. Shoppers call it the Coral Gables Sears, although the building sits just outside the Coral Gables city limits. In June 2024 Miami's Urban Development Review Board approved plans by RK Associates to replace the site with a mixed-use project of about 1,050 homes and ground-floor retail, Florida YIMBY reported, and SprawlTag described the store's future as uncertain in October 2026.

The same two decades emptied RadioShack and Circuit City, but neither had been the default American store for most of a century. Which raises the question of who is still paying the rent on these five.

Who owns Sears now, and what is Transform Holdco?

Sears belongs to Transform Holdco LLC, which trades as Transformco: a private company formed on February 11, 2019 and owned by ESL Investments, the hedge fund of Eddie Lampert, who is also Transformco's chairman and chief executive. It is based in Hoffman Estates, Illinois, the old Sears headquarters town.

Transformco runs the Sears and Kmart stores, Sears Home Services (appliance repair, HVAC, lawn and garden) and the Kenmore brand through a unit called Kenmore & Brands. Because it is private, it files no annual report, and no reliable Transformco revenue figure has been published. The last audited number for the business is Sears Holdings' $16.7 billion of revenue for fiscal 2017, down from $22.1 billion a year earlier.

Since 2019 Transformco has mostly sold things. It sold the DieHard battery brand to Advance Auto Parts for $200 million in December 2019, the Sears Outlet stores to Franchise Group in October 2019, and the Innovel Solutions delivery business, the old Sears Logistics, to Costco in March 2020.

It also bought back Sears Hometown, the small-town dealer chain, in October 2019. Sears Hometown filed for Chapter 11 on December 12, 2022 with about 121 locations, down from nearly 500, blaming its supplier in the filing: "lack of inventory has caused a rapid downward spiral that contributed to the filing of these Chapter 11 Cases," as Retail Dive reported in 2022. Those stores were liquidated, which leaves the question of how Lampert ended up holding all this in the first place.

Who owned Sears before Transformco?

Before 2019 Sears was owned by Sears Holdings Corporation, the public company formed when Kmart bought Sears, Roebuck and Co. in 2005, with Lampert as chairman. Before 2005, Sears, Roebuck was its own public company for most of the twentieth century.

Whether that merger ever had a chance is still argued. Mark Cohen, the former chief executive of Sears Canada, told CNBC in 2018 that Sears "was toast about a day after it closed" its 2005 merger with Kmart.

Sears Holdings filed for Chapter 11 in October 2018. On February 7, 2019, Judge Robert Drain approved ESL's $5.2 billion bid, which the Associated Press reported would keep about 425 stores open and roughly 45,000 people employed. Pressed on Lampert's role, Drain said: "He is a wealthy individual and a big boy," the AP reported in 2019.

Creditors later sued Lampert and ESL over pre-bankruptcy deals, including the Lands' End spin-off, and settled for $175 million in 2022 with no admission of liability, Bloomberg Law reported. That spin-off was one of dozens of exits, because Sears once owned far more than stores.

What companies did Sears own?

Sears owned an insurer, a brokerage, a credit card, a real estate chain, a clothing label and the best-known tool and battery brands in the country. Most left in a 1990s retreat into retailing; the rest were sold to pay for the decline.

The 1993 Allstate offering raised $2.4 billion and was, by Allstate's own account, the largest IPO in US history at the time. Two of the brand sales left a door open: Sears kept a perpetual license to sell Craftsman in its own stores, and a royalty-free license for DieHard products outside the car business. Kenmore is the one name still fully in the family, and it is the one most readers still own.

How to use Sears today: Kenmore, Craftsman and repairs

For a Kenmore or Craftsman appliance already in the house, the working routes in November 2026 are all online. Sears PartsDirect, a Transformco business, sells replacement parts by model number, and Sears Home Services still books in-home appliance repair; it was operating in 2025 with no sale completed, though its Puerto Rico service area closed on August 31, 2025.

New Kenmore appliances are coming back through licensing. Curtis International signed an exclusive North American license in September 2026 to make Kenmore refrigeration, small kitchen and other appliances, with the first products due in the fourth quarter of 2026. "For over one hundred years, Kenmore has been one of North America's most trusted names in quality appliances," Matthew Robbins, chief executive of Kenmore & Brands, said in the announcement, as HomePage News reported in 2026. Kenmore floor care is made separately under license and sold at Walmart.

New Craftsman tools now come from Stanley Black & Decker through other retailers, and new DieHard batteries from Advance Auto Parts. Two things are gone for good: the Sears Hometown stores, liquidated after 2022, and the Sears credit card, which Citibank replaced with Citi cards after April 2025. Sears.com still takes orders, which is a fitting place for the company to end up, given where it started.

The catalog that ran for 105 years

Richard Sears mailed his first catalog in 1888, a thin book of watches and jewelry, with ad copy he wrote himself promising "THE LOWEST PRICES ON EARTH," as Smithsonian magazine recounted in 2023.

The book grew into the Big Book, and between 1908 and 1940 it sold mail-order homes as numbered kits of lumber delivered by rail. Sears opened its first retail store in 1925, the stores slowly became the whole company, and the Big Book was retired on January 25, 1993, just as the web arrived.

So the line about Sears being Amazon before Amazon is not a flourish. Sears had the catalog, the warehouses and the national reach, and traded them for mall anchors; the company that rebuilt the model online only had to survive the dot-com crash to inherit it. On the five stores left, Dan Hamilton Rice, a marketing professor at Louisiana State University, told USA Today in 2025 what most of the industry assumes: "With so few stores, there's serious doubt from industry experts and former insiders that they could be profitable anymore."

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Sears stores are left?

Five full-line Sears department stores were left in the United States as of October 2026: Miami (Coral Way) and Orlando in Florida, Concord in California, Braintree in Massachusetts and El Paso in Texas. That is down from 547 full-line Sears stores in February 2018 and 8 in December 2024, and Transformco gives no notice before closing one, so call ahead.

Who owned Sears before Transformco?

Before February 2019, Sears was owned by Sears Holdings Corporation, the public company created when Kmart bought Sears, Roebuck and Co. in 2005. Eddie Lampert chaired Sears Holdings, and his hedge fund ESL Investments then bought the business out of bankruptcy for $5.2 billion, creating Transformco.

Can you still buy Kenmore appliances?

Yes, Kenmore is still a live brand in 2026, owned by Transformco and made by licensees rather than sold only at Sears. Curtis International signed an exclusive North American license in September 2026, with new Kenmore appliances due from the fourth quarter of 2026. Kenmore vacuums and floor care, made by a separate licensee, are sold at Walmart.
