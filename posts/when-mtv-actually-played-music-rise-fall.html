Who Owns MTV Now? Skydance After the Warner Deal

2026-03-26 | Updated 2026-11-06 | By Dana Reyes | 5 min read

MTV still exists in 2026, and its owner is Skydance. Paramount Skydance Corporation took that name in October 2026 after closing a Warner Bros. Discovery takeover worth about $110 billion with debt counted. The flagship channel still broadcasts in the United States and the United Kingdom, but five music-only channels went dark on December 31, 2025.

Is MTV still on the air in 2026?

Yes. On January 1, 2026 social media filled with posts saying MTV had gone off the air after 44 years, and Variety published a correction the same week. The flagship MTV channel kept broadcasting in the United States and the United Kingdom, and MTV2 still runs in the US.

What closed were the music-only channels. Five of them went dark in the UK on December 31, 2025: MTV Music, MTV 80s, MTV 90s, Club MTV and MTV Live. Music-only channels in Australia, Poland, France and Brazil closed in the same round.

A Paramount spokesperson told Variety in 2025 that "MTV's specialist music channels in the UK will no longer operate as linear channels," and said the company was adjusting its international pay TV portfolio as audiences moved to streaming and digital platforms. MTV and MTV2 in the United States were not part of that cut.

So the answer is narrower than the headlines suggested. MTV exists, and in the two biggest English-speaking markets it did not lose a channel at all. What stopped existing across most of the world at the end of 2025 was the version of MTV that ran videos back to back all day.

How did MTV end up inside Skydance?

MTV has changed parent companies twice in 14 months. Skydance Media and Paramount Global completed their merger on August 7, 2025, which created Paramount Skydance Corporation. That company then bought Warner Bros. Discovery and renamed itself Skydance in October 2026.

The Warner deal closed on October 6, 2026. Warner shareholders were paid $31 a share in cash, and the transaction came to roughly $110 billion once Warner's debt was counted. The shares now trade under the ticker SKYD, replacing the Paramount Skydance listing.

"Today is a historic day, not just for Skydance but for our entire industry," David Ellison said in a statement reported by Variety in 2026. Ellison is chairman and chief executive of the combined company, with former Mattel chief executive Ynon Kreiz as co-chief executive.

For MTV the practical question is cost rather than branding. The new company carries close to $80 billion in debt, and Ellison has projected at least $6 billion in savings. Cable channels that sell little advertising are the usual place a media company finds money like that, which is why every MTV schedule announcement now gets read as a clue.

What did MTV broadcast on its first day in 1981?

MTV launched at 12:01 a.m. Eastern on August 1, 1981. John Lack, the first chief operating officer of MTV Networks, delivered the opening line, "Ladies and gentlemen, rock and roll," over footage of a space shuttle launch.

The first video was "Video Killed the Radio Star" by the Buggles. The song was not even new, having been released as a single in 1979, and it aired twice in the channel's first 24 hours.

The channel reached only a handful of cable systems at the start, most of them in New Jersey, and record labels had to be talked into handing over videos for nothing. They were being asked to supply free programming to a channel almost nobody could watch.

That reluctance did not last long. Within a few years a premiere on MTV was how a record got launched rather than a place where a hit got repeated, and the Video Music Awards gave the channel an annual showcase of its own.

When did music videos actually disappear from MTV?

There is no single date, which is why people keep asking. Videos still filled up to eight hours a day in 2000. By 2008 the average was about three hours a day, much of it before breakfast, and through the 2010s even that thinned out.

The last daily countdown ended in the middle of that slide. Total Request Live launched on September 14, 1998, with Carson Daly counting down the ten most requested videos from a studio window above Times Square. MTV aired a three-hour finale, Total Finale Live, on November 16, 2008.

Two outside forces finished the job. Napster gutted the label revenue that paid for videos, and YouTube meant nobody had to sit through an hour of programming to see the one clip they wanted.

By the time streaming services arrived, the channel had already given up the format it was named after. The music-only spinoffs carried that job instead, which is what made their closure at the end of 2025 feel like the real ending.

What fills MTV's schedule in 2026?

Clip shows and reruns, mostly. MTV cancelled Ridiculousness in 2025 after 46 seasons and 14 years, a run of more than 1,700 episodes that had carried much of the channel's week for years. Reruns still air, and Variety tied the decision to cost cutting after the Skydance merger.

The one music night left is the Video Music Awards, and it no longer depends on MTV to find a crowd. The 2026 VMAs aired on September 27, 2026 on CBS and MTV together and drew about 8.4 million viewers, up roughly 51 percent on 2025 and the biggest audience the show had pulled since 2015.

The split inside that number is the story. CBS carried about 7.6 million of those viewers. The MTV simulcast accounted for about 686,000 of them. The largest music event on a channel named Music Television now needs a broadcast network to fill the room.

What survives of the channel's own past is mostly in private hands. People who taped MTV off cable in the 1990s now pay to digitize those VHS tapes, and the 90s websites still online often hold more of the era than the official archives kept.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns MTV now?

MTV is owned by Skydance, the company that was called Paramount Skydance Corporation until it renamed itself in October 2026 after buying Warner Bros. Discovery in a deal that closed on October 6, 2026. MTV sits in the same portfolio as CBS, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central and BET, and the shares trade under the ticker SKYD.

Did MTV shut down on New Year's Eve 2025?

No. The flagship MTV channel was still broadcasting in the United States and the United Kingdom through 2026, and Variety corrected the viral claim in January 2026. What closed on December 31, 2025 were five UK music-only channels, MTV Music, MTV 80s, MTV 90s, Club MTV and MTV Live, plus music-only channels in Australia, Poland, France and Brazil.

Does MTV still play music videos in 2026?

Barely. MTV never announced a stopping date, it tapered off: videos filled up to eight hours a day in 2000 and about three hours a day by 2008, and Total Request Live, the last daily video countdown, ended on November 16, 2008. In 2026 the MTV schedule runs on clip shows and reruns, and the Video Music Awards is its one music night of the year.
