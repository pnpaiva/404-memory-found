Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What We Collect

404 Memory Found is a nostalgia blog. We keep things simple. We collect anonymous visitor counts, the cookie choice you make on our banner, guestbook entries you choose to submit (name, email, message), and usage statistics from Google Analytics (pages visited, time on page, browser and approximate location, never your name).

Cookies

We use cookies and local storage to remember your cookie choice, your game scores and whether you have seen the welcome tutorial. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and measure which pages people read. Google AdSense, which shows the ads on this site, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or visit aboutads.info. If you choose Decline on our cookie banner, analytics and ad storage are switched off and Google shows only non-personalized ads.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase Realtime Database to store Snake game leaderboard scores globally. When you submit a Snake score, your chosen display name and score are stored. We use CounterAPI for anonymous visitor counting. Post images come from Wikimedia Commons and are stored on this site. Google (Analytics and AdSense) processes data as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your Data

We don't sell, trade, or share your personal information with anyone. Your guestbook entries are stored for display purposes only. You can request removal of your guestbook entry by emailing us.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at hello@404memoryfound.com.

This site is built for fun and nostalgia. We take your privacy seriously even if we don't take ourselves too seriously.
